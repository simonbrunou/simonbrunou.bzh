# Replace Iris with a recruiter-first, product-led design

Iris, the purple-to-pink design approved in May 2026 (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-05-16-engineering-elegant-redesign-design.md`), reads as AI-generated: its palette, gradient text, gradient washes, glow shadows, Inter body text, uppercase mono section labels and card grids are the defaults AI site generators produce. We replace it on all three surfaces (homepage, 404 page, CV) with a design built for the Visitor, a recruiter or client skimming for about 30 seconds. The featured projects (Diversif and Cinder) lead the homepage, followed by experience, a compact facts block and contact. Each surface commits to one colour mode with no theme toggle, Brittany appears as text only, and the Iris patterns above are banned.

Unlike the Iris spec, this is also a content change: the EN and FR copy is rewritten in first person, with facts instead of self-praise and French written as French, while dates, employers and stacks stay as they are.

## Considered options

- **Polish Iris.** Rejected: the AI look comes from the brief itself, so refining it keeps the look.
- **Brittany as the visual motif** (flag, triskell, granite and sea imagery). Rejected: regional symbols read as souvenir branding. The identity comes from the featured projects and from restraint instead.
